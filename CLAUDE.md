# CLAUDE.md

See README.md. Easy to get wrong:

- **`primers.tsv` and `amplicons.tsv` are curated by hand** from the protocols in `data/Arbeitshandbücher`; `docs/PRIMERS.md` is
  generated by `scripts/02_handbook_primers.py` and must not be edited. After changing a table run 02: it fails when a
  sequence is not in its protocol file.
- **Genomes are shared**, not owned: `scripts/01` writes into `data/gtdb_genomes/Mycobacteriaceae` (`GTDB_DIR`), the directory
  of `immensekansasii/scripts/01_gtdb_mycobacteria_genomes.sh`. Never delete or re-download there. Only genome directories are
  unpacked from a download (not the package README / md5sum / reports).
- **No Word converter on the cluster**: `cosmic_seq/handbook.py` reads `.docx` as zip and `.doc` as raw text runs. In `.doc`
  text the quote of `5'` is sometimes lost and the sequence starts on the next line.
- `cosmic_seq/insilico.py` started from `hsp65-refalign/hsp65_refalign/insilico.py` but returns all products and prefilters;
  it is not kept in sync with it.
- Direction in `primers.tsv` is the role in the pair (forward / reverse as the protocol names them), not the strand of the gene.
- The GTDB family Mycobacteriaceae includes Corynebacterium, Nocardia, Rhodococcus etc.; filter on `genus` for mycobacteria.
- `scripts/04` species groups = **NCBI** species groups + the curated `species_groups.tsv` (display labels; `taxid` rows
  make another NCBI taxon a group: genus Mycobacteroides 670516 = chelonae-abscessus complex; hand-listed kansasii
  complex and synonyms). GTDB is not used for groups (no complex rank). Species use GTDB's genus *Mycobacterium*
  (`cosmic_seq/names.py` maps Mycobacteroides etc.; keep its synonym list in sync with cosmic-files
  `MYCOBACTERIUM_SYNONYMS`). One group per species: the output is the species -> group lookup of cosmic-files; a curated
  species already in an NCBI group needs `override` in its note.
- Step 3 runs the GTDB representatives **plus** the curated `extra_genomes.tsv` (taxa GTDB merges, e.g. the
  M. abscessus subspecies bolletii / massiliense; label = species in the FASTA). Add genomes there, not to the representatives.
- **Environment:** `env_cosmic` (`environment.yml`). Run step 3 through `sbatch submit_references.sbatch`, not on the login node.
- **Type check:** `~/data/conda/envs/env_typecheck/bin/mypy` (strict).
- **Outputs** go to `output/cosmic-seq/results/` on the shares, not the repo.
- **Paths:** `cosmic_seq/kansasii_paths.py` is a vendored copy of `kansasii-docs/templates/kansasii_paths.py`; never edit it here
  (`bash repos/check_sync.sh --fix` restores it).
